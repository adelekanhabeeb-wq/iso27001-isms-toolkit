## 1. Purpose & Leadership Commitment (Clause 5.2)
**CloudPulse Ltd** is committed to protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of all customer, employee, and corporate information assets. Executive leadership fully supports the Information Security Management System (ISMS) aligned with **ISO/IEC 27001:2022** and provides the resources required to maintain it.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all CloudPulse Ltd employees, contractors, temporary staff, and third-party suppliers who access corporate systems, source code, or customer data within the defined ISMS scope (`ISMS-SCP-001`).

## 3. Core Security Principles
To achieve our information security objectives, CloudPulse Ltd enforces the following principles:

- **Risk-Based Governance:** Security controls are selected and prioritized using a formal, repeatable risk assessment methodology (`ISMS-RSK-002`).
- **Least Privilege & Zero Trust:** Access to production infrastructure, source code, and customer data is granted strictly on a least-privilege, role-based need-to-know basis (`Annex A.5.15`, `A.8.2`).
- **Security by Design:** Information security requirements are embedded into the software development lifecycle (SDLC), architecture reviews, and change management workflows (`Annex A.8.25`, `A.8.27`).
- **Continuous Compliance & Monitoring:** Technical controls are continuously monitored through automated cloud posture checks, centralized logging, and vulnerability scanning (`Annex A.8.15`, `A.8.16`).
- **Legal & Regulatory Compliance:** All operations comply with applicable laws, including UK GDPR, EU GDPR, and contractual client SLAs (`Annex A.5.31`, `A.5.34`).

## 4. Roles & Responsibilities

| Role | Information Security Responsibilities |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Chief Executive Officer (CEO)** | Holds ultimate accountability for the ISMS, approves the Information Security Policy, and ensures adequate budget and resourcing. |
| **Information Security Lead** | Manages day-to-day ISMS operations, coordinates risk assessments, maintains the Statement of Applicability, and leads internal/external audits. |
| **Engineering & DevOps Leads** | Implement technical Annex A controls across AWS infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, and production applications. |
| **All Personnel** | Comply with all security policies, complete annual security awareness training, and immediately report suspected security incidents. |

## 5. Policy Compliance & Enforcement
Compliance with this policy is mandatory. Violations may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contract, and legal action where applicable.
